Sampling sticky Brownian motion and its local time

The pages and movies of a talk at the AMS Fall Western Sectional Meeting, Arizona State University, November 8, 2026, by C. Mount. They accompany the paper of C. Mount and I. Fatkullin, Exact bridges for sticky reflected Brownian motion (to appear on arXiv).

One path, refined

Click on a bridge of sticky reflected Brownian motion to query it at that time. The samplers of the paper, ported to JavaScript, draw the position and the local time there from their exact law given everything drawn before.

The zero set of reflected Brownian motion, and its local time

Drag each 𝜀 and watch the approximations of the zero set and of the local time close in on their limits.

A reflecting wall and a sticky wall

A heavy particle moves only because the light molecules of a gas hit it. On the right, the trace shows its distance to the wall. The first wall reflects the particle. The second wall holds it in a shallow well until a hard enough hit frees it, and the trace marks the time held in red.

One path, refined (the movie)

The samplers refine one bridge of sticky reflected Brownian motion with about 500 queries. The red bars mark the times not yet inside a found excursion. Their length decreases to the time the path spends at 0.
