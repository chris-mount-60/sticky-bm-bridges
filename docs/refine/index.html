One path, refined

A bridge of sticky reflected Brownian motion 𝔜 with stickiness μ, from x to y over [0, T], with local time r at 0 by time T. Click on the picture to query the path at that time. Each click draws the position Yu and the local time Lu from their exact law given everything drawn before, from a few uniform, normal and exponential variables. No time step and no approximation enter.

The skeleton

What the picture shows

The sampler keeps a skeleton of the path, the times it has queried with the position and the local time at each, together with the zeros it has drawn. Between two recorded times the local time either stays constant, and then the path is a Bessel(3) bridge away from 0, or it grows, and then both ends sit at 0. The red bars mark the intervals where it grows. They hold the zero set of the path, and their total length decreases to its Lebesgue measure μr as the skeleton grows. A query in a red bar that lands above 0 also draws the start and the end of its excursion, so the new excursion leaves the red at once.

The bridge

The ends must satisfy μr < T, and r = 0 needs x + y > 0. With the box ticked, the page first draws r from the law of the local time given the two positions, by a sampler that is not in the paper, and the picture is then a bridge given positions only. The seed and the sequence of queries fix the path, so the same seed and the same clicks give the same picture.

Keys 1 (random query), U (undo), N (new path).

The samplers of Algorithms 1 to 3 are a line-by-line port to JavaScript of code/sticky_bridge.py, the code of the paper (sampler.js). The sampler behind the box above is in neither the paper nor that code. C. Mount and I. Fatkullin, Exact bridges for sticky reflected Brownian motion, arXiv (to appear).
