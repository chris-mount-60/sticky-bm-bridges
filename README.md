# Exact bridges for sticky reflected Brownian motion

This repository holds the interactive pages and the movies of the talk *Sampling sticky Brownian motion and its local
time*, given at the AMS Fall Western Sectional Meeting at Arizona State University on November 8, 2026. They accompany
the paper

C. Mount and I. Fatkullin, *Exact bridges for sticky reflected Brownian motion* (2026, to appear on arXiv).

The pages are served at https://chris-mount-60.github.io/. *One path, refined* lets you query a bridge
of sticky reflected Brownian motion by clicking on it, with the samplers of the paper ported to JavaScript. *The zero
set of reflected Brownian motion, and its local time* shows the approximations of the zero set and of the local time
closing in on their limits. The movies in `docs/movies` show a heavy particle in a gas of light molecules above a
reflecting wall and above a sticky wall, and one sticky path refined by the samplers.
